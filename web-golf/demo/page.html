Hole 1

How a page becomes a course

The script reads every element on the page, measures where it sits, and gives it a role. Long paragraphs like this one are rough, so a putt through them dies fast. Play along the margins or over the gaps between lines.

Headings, big type
Walls. The ball bounces off.
Body text
Rough. Slows the ball about 4×.
Images, icons
Bumpers. They add speed.
ASCII art
Rails and pegs, one per glyph.
Inputs, Buy, Delete
Water. One stroke penalty.
Launch, Play, Sign up
The cups, one hole each.
Hole 2

The bumper garden

Round images become round bumpers. Bank off one at full power and it fires the ball back out faster than it came in.

Hole 3

Water hazard

Form fields are water, and so is any button that buys, deletes or aborts something. Splash in and you go back to where you hit from, plus one stroke.

This form is part of the course. Submitting it sends nothing.

Also water. It deletes nothing.
Hole 4

Take it to any site

  1. Drag the ⛳ Web Golf chip at the top of this page onto your bookmarks bar.
  2. If dragging doesn't work, copy the code below, add a new bookmark, and paste it in as the URL.
  3. Open any website and click the bookmark. The page's buttons become the holes.
